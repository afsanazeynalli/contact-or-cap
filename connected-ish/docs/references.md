# Scientific references

These references provide scientific context for specific components of `connected-ish`. They do not constitute validation of the software or of any field interpretation.

1. Archie, G. E. (1942). The Electrical Resistivity Log as an Aid in Determining Some Reservoir Characteristics. *Transactions of the AIME, 146*, 54–62. https://doi.org/10.2118/942054-G
   - Context: resistivity-based reservoir characterization and saturation interpretation.

2. Kashib, T., & Srinivasan, S. (2006). A probabilistic approach to integrating dynamic data in reservoir models. *Journal of Petroleum Science and Engineering, 50*(3–4), 241–257. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.petrol.2005.11.002
   - Context: probabilistic integration of dynamic observations and uncertainty.

3. Thiam, M., & Nakhaee, A. (2023). Reservoir interwell connectivity estimation from small datasets using a probabilistic data driven approach and uncertainty quantification. *Geoenergy Science and Engineering, 230*, 212154. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geoen.2023.212154
   - Context: interwell connectivity estimation and uncertainty quantification.

4. Integrate inter–well connectivity data with static reservoir models based on Bayesian formalism. (2023). *Petroleum Research, 8*(3), 433–438. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ptlrs.2023.01.003
   - Context: Bayesian integration of dynamic connectivity information with static models.

5. Tang, H., & Durlofsky, L. J. (2024). Graph network surrogate model for subsurface flow optimization. *Journal of Computational Physics, 512*, 113132. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2024.113132
   - Context: graph/network representations for subsurface flow and optimization.

6. Song, H., Zhu, J., Wei, C., Wang, J., Du, S., & Xie, C. (2022). Data-driven physics-informed interpolation evolution combining historical-predicted knowledge for remaining oil distribution prediction. *Journal of Petroleum Science and Engineering, 217*, 110795. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.petrol.2022.110795
   - Context: physics-informed and data-driven prediction of remaining oil.

7. Bukhanov, N., Subbotina, M., Voskresenkiy, A., & Katterbauer, K. (2024). Geological and dynamic similarity for reservoir state prediction by well connectivity. *Geoenergy Science and Engineering, 234*, 212667. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geoen.2024.212667
   - Context: well-network/connectivity representations combining geological and dynamic information.

8. Static connectivity of fluvial reservoirs and their temporal evolution: An example from densely drilled subsurface data in the Sanzhao Sag, Songliao Basin. (2021). *Marine and Petroleum Geology, 134*, 105327. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpetgeo.2021.105327
   - Context: static connectivity and its evolution in fluvial reservoir architecture.

9. Passalacqua, P. (2017). The Delta Connectome: A network-based framework for studying connectivity in river deltas. *Geomorphology, 277*, 50–62. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2016.04.001
   - Context: network-based connectivity concepts for deltaic systems.

10. Yousefzadeh, R., Kazemi, A., Al-Hmouz, R., & Al-Moosawi, I. (2025). Determination of optimal oil well placement using deep learning under geological uncertainty. *Geoenergy Science and Engineering, 246*, 213621. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geoen.2024.213621
   - Context: data-driven well-placement optimization under geological uncertainty.

11. Physics-informed machine learning in petroleum engineering: advances, applications, and future directions. (2026). *Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation, 163*(3), 110610. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cnsns.2026.110610
   - Context: current PIML applications and limitations in petroleum engineering.
